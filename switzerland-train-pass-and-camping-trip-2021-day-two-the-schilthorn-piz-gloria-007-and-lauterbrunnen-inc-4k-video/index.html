Switzerland Train Pass & Camping Trip - 2021: Day Two – The Schilthorn, Piz Gloria, 007 & Lauterbrunnen (Inc. 4K Video)

Photos and video from my 11 day trip to Switzerland in the Autumn of 2021.

Day two and as the weather is holding up nicely I decide to tackle my next big destination today – the summit of the Schilthorn! made famous for being the film location for the iconic Bond movie “On Her Majesty’s Secret Service” which was released in 1969 and has very interesting story behind how it happened.

To reach the Schilthorn and Piz Gloria (the 360-degree revolving restaurant that sits on top of the summit) I took a train from Interlaken to Stechelberg then took a series of five cable cars to reach the summit, the whole journey was full of eye watering scenery! enjoy the photos! 🙂
