Switzerland Train Pass & Camping Trip - 2021: Interlaken (Inc. 4K Video)

Photos from my 11 day trip to Switzerland in the Autumn of 2021.

The majority of my stay in Switzerland was in Interlaken and this was my base for the first seven days of which I spent camping at Camping Hobby and used my Swiss Travel Pass to visit each day different places around Switzerland using the excellent train service they run, the last three days I stayed in Lausanne and a cheap hotel (cheapest I could find for Switzerland prices!).

This is my second time I have visited Interlaken, once in early 2000s and its as beautiful now as it was then! – enjoy the photos! 🙂
