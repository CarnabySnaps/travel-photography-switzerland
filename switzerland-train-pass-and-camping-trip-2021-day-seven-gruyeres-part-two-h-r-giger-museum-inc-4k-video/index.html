Switzerland Train Pass & Camping Trip - 2021: Day Seven – Gruyeres – Part Two – H. R. Giger Museum (Inc. 4K Video)

Photos and video from my 11 day trip to Switzerland in the Autumn of 2021.

Day seven and I travel to the Swiss French town of Gruyeres leaving Interlaken and the camping part of my trip, I visited this medieval town en-route to my final destination of Lausanne.

What enticed me to the charming town Gruyeres was not only the beautiful location in the Swiss countryside but also the idea of visiting the one and only Swiss surrealist painter, sculptor and set designer HR Giger’s museum and Giger bar, a permanent repository of his work!

Under the late Giger’s wishes, photography and filming was not allowed inside the exhibition (that has now been permanently burned into my brain! 😉 ) but I was allowed to photograph the shop area and the sculptures outside the Saint-Germain Castle that was brought by Giger to house the museum and the bar  – enjoy the photos! 🙂
