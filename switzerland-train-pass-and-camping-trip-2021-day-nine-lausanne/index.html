Switzerland Train Pass & Camping Trip - 2021: Day Nine – Lausanne

Photos from my 11 day trip to Switzerland in the Autumn of 2021.

Day nine and my last full day in Switzerland and as the weather was a bit off today I took a walk around Lausanne and discovered that my covid pass I have from England is not recognised in Switzerland,  just the day before the country had put in tighter restrictions and now to visit museums/ exhibitions or eat in restaurants the new Euro pass was required so my options where a bit limited but as it was the end of my trip I was not too bothered and enjoyed checking out a spot by Lake Geneva that I have visited on two other past trips to Lausanne! – enjoy the photos! 🙂
