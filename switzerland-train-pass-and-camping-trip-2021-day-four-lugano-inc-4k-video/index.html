Switzerland Train Pass & Camping Trip - 2021: Day Four – Lugano (Inc. 4K Video)

Photos and video from my 11 day trip to Switzerland in the Autumn of 2021.

Day four and today the weather takes a turn for the worst! after a rainy nights camping I awake to more rain in Interlaken and decide that as I’ve got the Swiss Travel Pass I would travel quite a distance to the Italian Swiss town of Lugano – the climate there is more of a sunny one for sure!

It takes an average of 4h 42m to travel from Interlaken West to Lugano by train, over a distance of around 70 miles (113 km) so I brought my lunch on the train with me! – enjoy the photos! 🙂
